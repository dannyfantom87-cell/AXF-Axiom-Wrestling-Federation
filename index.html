About AXF

The Federation
Built on Reckoning

Axiom Wrestling Federation is the premier global wrestling promotion — a universe where corporate power struggles, legacy wars, and generational conflicts play out on the grandest stage. We do not produce matches. We produce history.

Operating across two weekly flagship shows — RAW and SmackDown — AXF delivers serialized sports drama at the highest level. Every championship reign is earned. Every storyline has a destination. Every loss has a purpose.

👑
Power
Control of the board. Control of the narrative. Control of the future.
📜
Legacy
Every match, every promo builds the archive of history.
🎬
Cinema
Wrestling is a sport. AXF is prestige drama. We book like both.
⚡
Destiny
WrestleMania is the destination. Every story traces back to it.
